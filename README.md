### 👋 A bit about me

I'm someone who likes figuring out how things work, then building something better than what I found. Most of that curiosity lands on AI and web development right now — but it started long before any of that, and it'll probably move somewhere new eventually.

- 🧠 I enjoy turning fuzzy ideas into working products more than I enjoy talking about them
- 🎨 I care about how things *feel* to use, not just whether they work
- 🤝 Genuinely enjoy collaborating — good ideas get better with more than one head on them
- ⚡ Learn fastest by building, breaking, and rebuilding
- 📍 Bengaluru, India · Scaler School of Technology
